How the No Excuse Method Works

The No Excuse Debt Elimination Method is a more aggressive version of the traditional debt snowball. Instead of keeping your extra payment fixed, your margin grows automatically every time you eliminate a debt.

  • 🔹 Pay the minimum payment on every debt.
  • 🔹 Apply your extra margin to the smallest active debt.
  • 🔹 When that debt is paid off, its minimum payment + your margin roll forward to the next debt.
  • 🔹 Your margin grows larger with every debt you eliminate—creating unstoppable momentum.
